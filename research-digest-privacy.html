Privacy Policy

Research Digest — 주간 연구동향 메일 자동 발송 (개인용)

Effective date: 2026-10-19

What Research Digest is

Research Digest is a personal automation run by Seunghoo Jeong. Once a week it collects metadata of newly published academic papers from public sources (OpenAlex, arXiv, Semantic Scholar), summarizes them, and e-mails the digest to the owner's own Gmail account. It is not offered to, and cannot be used by, anyone else.

Google user data accessed

The app requests a single Google OAuth scope, https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.send, and uses it only to send the weekly digest from the owner's account to the owner's account. It does not read, search, store, or modify any e-mail or other Google account data.

Storage and sharing

The OAuth token is kept only on the owner's computer and as an encrypted GitHub Actions secret in a private repository, and is used solely to send the digest. Google user data is not sold, shared with, or transferred to any third party, and is not used for advertising or to train AI models.

Other services

Public paper metadata (titles, authors, abstracts) is sent to the Anthropic Claude API to generate summaries, and the resulting digest is saved to the owner's Notion workspace. No Google user data is sent to these services.

Limited Use

Research Digest's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at Google Account → Third-party apps & services.
